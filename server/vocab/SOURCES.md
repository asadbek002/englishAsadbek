# So'z ro'yxati manbalari

`cefr-words.tsv` quyidagi ochiq ro'yxatlar asosida tuzilgan (artikl va yordamchi fe'llar olib tashlangan,
bir xil so'zning turli so'z turkumlari birlashtirilgan, eng past daraja qoldirilgan):

- **CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5** (A1–B2). Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies.
  https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj — tadqiqot va tijorat maqsadlarida bepul, manbani ko'rsatish sharti bilan.
- **Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 (ver 1.0)** by Octanove Labs.
  https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj — Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
  Shu sababli `cefr-words.tsv` fayli ham CC BY-SA 4.0 litsenziyasi ostida tarqatiladi.

O'zbekcha va ruscha tarjimalar `server/scripts/vocab.ts` skripti orqali AI yordamida yaratiladi.
